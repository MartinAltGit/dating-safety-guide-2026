SMS channel · 18+

Scam text messages: the thread is the door. Stay on the app.

A practical handbook for SMS that turned into a romance pitch, a “sorry, wrong number,” a package link, or a trading screenshot. This is the text channel — not a second catalog of first money-ask scripts. Save the evidence. Do not reply with a code. Report the number and the profile.

Date Safely editors Updated Sep 9, 2026 18+

Quick answer

Scam text messages in a dating context are a channel, not a personality test. Four usual doors: romance bait, a wrong-number opener, a package or delivery pretext, and an investment pitch over SMS. Do not click the link. Do not send a one-time code. Screenshot the thread, forward spam to 7726 in the US, and if a match moved you here, report them in the original app. How the first money ask sounds belongs on romance scam examples — this page stays on the text. Hub: romance scams explained. Numbers: scam phone numbers.

Key takeaways

  • SMS has no useful report button. That is why crews leave Tinder, Bumble, and Hinge.
  • A wrong-number text that becomes a relationship is a sourcing trick, not fate.
  • Delivery and bank “links” in a personal thread are pretext. Open the official app yourself.
  • Save first, then block. Deleted chats are how the second story (“I never texted you”) works.

This is the text channel — not the scripts page

People search scam text messages after a thread they cannot quite name. Sometimes it is a carrier spam blast. Sometimes it is a match who “hates the app.” This page is the SMS surface: how the message arrived, what the pretext is, what to save, and where to file. It is not a list of hospital / travel / gift-card lines. Those first money-ask examples live on romance scam examples and romance scam scripts.

Romance fraud can start on a dating app, Instagram, or a text. The crime is the fake relationship plus a payment rail. Types map: online dating scams. App-specific match-to-WhatsApp patterns: dating app scams.

Four text-channel patterns

Romance bait

A warm text from someone you never matched — a compliment, a “you look like someone I know,” a photo that may be stolen or generated. The goal is a private thread they control. Days later comes the crisis, the ticket, or the trading screenshot. If you have not done unscripted live video on a platform that can ban them, treat money talk as the end. Hub: romance scams explained. Long crypto version: pig butchering on dating apps.

Wrong-number opener

They “meant” someone else. They apologize. They ask what you do. The politeness is the product. A real misdial ends. A sourcing text continues, then asks for WhatsApp, a photo, or a call. You do not owe a stranger a biography because they claimed a typo. Do not send nudes to “prove” you are real — that can become sextortion.

Package / delivery pretext

A carrier, “USPS,” Amazon, or “your porch package” link. Sometimes it arrives after a dating chat (“I sent you something”). The site wants a card, a login, or a one-time code. Open the store or carrier app you already have — do not use the link in the text. A match who needs you to “pay the customs fee” on a gift is running a money ask, not shipping. Military / customs costume: military romance scams.

Investment pitch via SMS

Friendly check-ins, then a screenshot of a dashboard, a “mentor,” or a coin that “already 3x’d.” A small withdrawal that arrives is the hook. Unlock fees and taxes to release a frozen balance are the second steal. If a person you have not met starts talking wallets, stop. You do not owe them a debate about charts. Hub: pig butchering scams.

STOP is for lists you joined. It is not a conversation with a stranger.

Replying to a random long-code romance or delivery text confirms the number is live. Do not argue. Do not joke. Screenshot, forward, block.

Evidence to keep

Delete later if you want. Save first. Threads vanish when they block you, and “I never texted you” is easier if you have nothing.

  • Full-thread screenshots — include the number or short code at the top, timestamps, and any link (copy the URL into a note; do not tap it again).
  • The dating profile if this started as a match — username, photos, bio — before you report.
  • Payment crumbs — gift-card receipts, wallet addresses, Cash App / Zelle names, fake “broker” URLs.
  • Short code vs long number — a five- or six-digit code can still be abused; a regular mobile is easier to rotate. Note both.

If intimate images are in the thread and they threaten to leak them, that is sextortion. Do not pay. Playbook: sextortion help.

Stay on the app

A dating app can ban an account. SMS cannot. “Text me, the app is broken” is the usual line. A real match can keep talking in the original thread until unscripted live video — wave a hand, turn, hold up something you name. Voice notes and gym clips are not that check.

If they already have your number, you can still refuse the rail: no codes, no gift cards, no “quick” crypto. Move the conversation back to the app or end it. Walk-away list: online dating red flags · dating scam red flags.

Reporting

Do not reply with a code or a link tap

Do not call the number in the text either. Phone workflow: scam phone numbers.

Forward and block

US: forward the SMS to 7726 (SPAM), then block. Tell Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile, or your MVNO. Use the phone’s junk-text controls so the next one files quietly.

Report the dating profile

If this started as a swipe, report and block in the app. Blocking without reporting leaves them in other people’s stacks. Checklist: how to report a dating scam.

File if money or threats are involved

US: ReportFraud.ftc.gov and IC3.gov. UK: Action Fraud. Australia: Scamwatch / ReportCyber. If cash left, call the bank the same day and skip anyone who offers paid “recovery.” Get money back from a scam.

FAQ

What do scam text messages look like in dating?

Romance bait, a wrong-number opener that keeps chatting, a package link, or an investment screenshot after friendly SMS. Stay on the original app until live video. Hub: romance scams.

What is a wrong-number text scam?

A “sorry, meant someone else” text that becomes a relationship. The goal is a private thread. If money appears, it is romance fraud that started on SMS. Do not send photos or codes.

Should I reply to a scam text?

No. A reply confirms the number is live. Screenshot, forward to 7726 in the US, and block. Do not tap the link or send a one-time code.

How do I report scam text messages?

Save screenshots, forward to 7726, tell the carrier, report the dating profile if there is one, then file FTC ReportFraud and IC3 when money or blackmail is involved. Steps: report a dating scam.
