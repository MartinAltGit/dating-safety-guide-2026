How to cancel a dating app subscription

One rule covers Tinder Gold, Bumble Premium, and Hinge+: cancel in the store that billed you. This is the overview; each app has its own taps.

Date Safely editors Updated Aug 17, 2026 18+

Quick answer

Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. iPhone purchases cancel in Settings → Subscriptions. Android purchases cancel in Google Play. Website purchases cancel in that site’s account.

Key takeaways

  • Find the receipt: Apple, Google, or the dating site’s email.
  • Cancel at least a day before renewal.
  • You usually keep paid features until the period ends.
  • Refunds are a separate, rarer path.

FAQ

How do I cancel a dating app subscription?

Cancel where you paid. iPhone: Settings → name → Subscriptions. Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions. Website: that site’s account email. Deleting the app never stops the bill.
