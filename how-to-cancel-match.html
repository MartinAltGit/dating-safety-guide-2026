How to Cancel Match.com Subscription & Auto-Renewal

Match.com subscriptions auto-renew and can be tricky to cancel. Here's exactly how to end your subscription and stop future charges.

Why Cancellation Matters

We researched Match.com's official help pages and consumer reports on its cancellation flow, including the phone number and live chat options.

Step-by-Step Cancellation

Cancel via website

Log in at match.com → click your profile → Help / Settings → Manage Subscription / Cancel → follow the prompts and confirm.

Cancel by phone

Call Match.com customer service using the number in your account. Ask to cancel the auto-renewal and get a confirmation number.

Dispute surprise renewals

If billed after cancellation, contact support or your bank. See our charged after cancellation guide.

Verify cancellation

Save the confirmation email or number. Check no future payment is scheduled in your Match account.
