Online dating red flags: a practical safety checklist for 2026

Not a scare page. The tells that show up before money leaves — and the ordinary pressure flags that mean slow down even when nobody asked for a wallet yet.

Date Safely editors Updated Sep 9, 2026 18+

Quick answer

Walk away when they rush you off-app, ask for money or crypto, or refuse unscripted live video. Love bombing, isolation, and stories that slip are also dating red flags — even before a payment ask. One strong flag is enough. Shorter scam overview: dating scam red flags. Longer romance list: romance scam checklist.

Key takeaways

  • Online dating red flags are a walk-away list, not a quiz you must finish.
  • Money, gift cards, wires, and crypto from someone you have not met = end of chat.
  • Refuse live video or only send clips — treat that as incomplete identity.
  • If cash already left: stop, bank same day, report.

How to use this checklist

Searchers type dating red flags and online dating red flags for two overlapping reasons: they sense a scam, or they sense a bad dynamic. This page covers both. Romance fraud still follows a boring funnel — polished profile, private chat, crisis or “opportunity” — described in the romance scams hub. Ordinary pressure (control, isolation, moving too fast) is not always crime, but it is still a reason to pause.

You do not need every item. Two weak flags might mean “slow down.” One money ask is enough to stop.

Chat and contact flags

  • Rush to WhatsApp, Telegram, or iMessage in the first days — “the app keeps logging me out.” Report buttons stay behind.
  • Refuse to stay on the original app until live video or a real meet plan.
  • Odd hours and a job story that blocks every meet — military, oil, overseas work used only to explain distance and sudden fees.
  • Copy-paste warmth that ignores what you just said, or English that flips between perfect and broken depending on the topic.

Money and crypto flags

  • Any ask for cash, gift cards, wires, Zelle, Cash App, or crypto from someone you have not met in person.
  • “Just this once” hospital, travel, visa, or customs fees — the first ask is designed to sound temporary. Catalog: romance scam scripts.
  • A trading screenshot, wallet address, or unlock fee — that path is pig butchering, not a hot tip.
  • Pressure to keep the payment secret from family or friends.

Identity and video flags

  • Refuse unscripted live video, or only send gym clips and “broken camera” excuses for a week.
  • Blank reverse image search — stolen photos still light up; AI faces often do not. A blank result is inconclusive, not a green light. AI dating scams.
  • A link to “verify” on another site — fake exclusive apps steal cards.
  • Profile photos that never match the person on a call — even slightly.

How to run a silly live check without turning it into a lecture: video chat safety.

Pressure and relationship flags

  • Love bombing — “soulmate” language in days, especially when paired with a crisis only money solves.
  • Isolation — “don’t tell your family, they won’t understand us.”
  • Story details that slip while the money or control pitch stays smooth.
  • Anger or guilt when you set a boundary — staying on-app, no money, public first meet.
  • Push to meet privately too soon or to skip telling a friend where you are. Meet habits: first date safety.

You do not owe them a debate.

Real people who want a relationship can handle “I stay on the app until live video” and “I do not send money to people I have not met.” If that boundary ends the chat, the chat needed to end. Longer romance-specific list: romance scam red flags checklist.

What to do instead

Stay on the app early

Use the report button where the chat lives. Off-app later — after live video and a real plan — is fine. Day-one Telegram is not.

Live video with a silly ask

Wave a hand, turn sideways, hold up something you name. Pre-recorded clips are not verification.

Never send money

Travel, hospital, visa, investment “test,” unlock fee — all hard stops. If you already sent: how to report a dating scam.

Tell someone you trust

Shame is part of many scripts. A friend reduces the chance you send again under pressure.

FAQ

What are the biggest online dating red flags in 2026?

Rush off-app before live video, any money or crypto ask, refuse unscripted live video, love bombing paired with a crisis only money solves, isolation, and a story that slips while the payment pitch stays smooth. Overview hub: romance scams.

Is moving to WhatsApp always a red flag?

Not always — but early off-app chat is the most common next step in romance scams. Stay on the app until live video and a real meet plan. If they push hard on day one, treat that as data. Shorter list: dating scam red flags.

What should I do if I already see several dating red flags?

Pause or end the chat. One strong flag is enough. If money left, stop sending, call the bank the same day, then FTC ReportFraud and IC3 (US). Steps: report a dating scam.

Are dating red flags the same as romance scam red flags?

They overlap. Scam checklists focus on money, off-app, and fake identity. This page also covers control and pressure that may not be a crime yet. Use both: romance scam checklist and this walk-away list.
