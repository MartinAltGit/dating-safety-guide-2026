How to Cancel Dating App Subscriptions: Full Guide 2026

Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription. That is the single most expensive misunderstanding in dating apps — and the reason people keep paying for months after they stop swiping. This site walks through the actual cancellation path for each major service, on each platform.

Why you can trust this guide

Every cancellation path here was verified against the apps' own help pages and the official Apple, Google Play, and web billing flows as of August 2026. We are independent and not affiliated with any dating service — policies change, so always confirm in the app's own settings.

The universal rule

Where you paid determines where you cancel:

You paid viaCancel in
Apple App StoreiPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions
Google PlayPlay Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions
The service's websiteAccount settings on the website

Cancelling stops the next charge. You usually keep premium features until the end of the period already paid for, and refunds for unused time are rare.
