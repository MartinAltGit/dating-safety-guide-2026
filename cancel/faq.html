Dating App Subscription FAQ: Cancellations, Refunds, Charges

Quick answers to the questions people actually search when a dating subscription goes wrong.

Does deleting the app cancel my subscription?

No. This is the most common mistake. The subscription lives with the app store (Apple/Google) or the service's website billing — not with the app on your phone. Cancel in the store's subscription settings or the service's account settings.

Does deleting my profile cancel the subscription?

Usually no. Account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate actions on most services. Cancel the subscription first, then delete the profile if you're leaving.

Will I get money back if I cancel mid-month?

Generally no — most terms say no refunds for unused time. Exceptions: accidental same-day renewals (contact support immediately), unauthorized charges (dispute with your bank), and app-store refund requests within ~48 hours (case-by-case). Details in the refund guide.

I cancelled but got charged again. What now?

  1. Check where you cancelled — cancelling in the wrong place is the usual cause.
  2. Screenshot the cancellation confirmation and the new charge.
  3. Contact the service's support first; if unresolved, dispute the charge with your bank/card issuer.

How do I stop free trials turning into paid subscriptions?

Set a calendar reminder for 2 days before the trial converts, or cancel immediately after starting the trial — most services let you keep trial benefits until the end date even after cancelling.

Which cancellation is hardest to undo financially?

Long-cycle plans (3 or 6 months billed upfront). Always check the renewal cycle before paying; a "monthly-feeling" price can be a quarterly charge.
