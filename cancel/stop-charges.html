Dating App Refunds: What Actually Works in 2026

"How do I stop dating apps from charging me?" is one of the most common subscription complaints. Here is the complete stop-the-charges procedure, in order.

Step 1: Find every active subscription

  • iPhone: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions. Every auto-renewing subscription is listed with its renewal date.
  • Android: Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Card statements: search your bank/card history for the service name or its billing entity (many dating companies bill under a parent-company name, not the app name).

Step 2: Cancel each one where it lives

App-store subscriptions are cancelled in the store. Website subscriptions are cancelled in the service's account settings — look for "Auto-Renew" and switch it off at least 24 hours before renewal.

Step 3: Collect proof

Screenshot the cancellation confirmation and the "Expires on" state. This is what wins disputes if a charge still lands.

Step 4: If a charge still appears

  1. Request a refund through the store first (Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com; Google Play: support refund flow). Best odds within 48 hours of the charge.
  2. Contact the service's support the same day — some issue goodwill refunds for accidental renewals.
  3. Still charged after a confirmed cancellation? Dispute with your card issuer with the screenshots as evidence.

Why charges keep coming

Almost always one of three causes: the app was deleted instead of the subscription (deleting the app does nothing), auto-renew was left on for website billing, or the subscription sits under a different Apple/Google account than the one being checked.
