How to Cancel Badoo Premium — 2026

Badoo Premium renews automatically. Cancel where you originally paid:

iPhone

  1. Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Badoo → Cancel Subscription

Android

  1. Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Badoo → Cancel subscription

Web

  1. Log in → settings → Premium / payment management → cancel auto-renewal

What to check after cancelling

  • The store shows the subscription as "Expires on …" rather than "Renews on …"
  • You receive a cancellation confirmation (email or in-app)
  • Keep a screenshot — it is your proof if a charge still appears
