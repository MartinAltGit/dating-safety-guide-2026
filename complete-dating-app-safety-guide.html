FAQ

How do I cancel Tinder Gold on iPhone?

Open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, select Tinder, then Cancel Subscription. Deleting the Tinder app does not stop Gold, Plus, or Platinum billing. Apple is the merchant for most iPhone purchases.

Does a reverse image search catch fake dating profiles?

It catches stolen photos. AI-generated faces are unique, so Google Images and TinEye often return nothing. Treat a blank search as inconclusive and ask for a live video call on the app.

What is a pig butchering scam on a dating app?

A long romance con that starts on a dating app, moves to WhatsApp or Telegram, then steers you to a fake crypto or investment platform. Any match who introduces trading or wallets is a walk-away.

Is Tinder Gold worth it?

Gold’s main extra is seeing who liked you. Plus is extra likes. Platinum is the rest of the upsell. The trap is the 6-month auto-renew, not the badge. Screenshot the full price table in your store.
