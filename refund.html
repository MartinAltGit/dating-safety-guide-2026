Refunds · 18+

Refunds are rare

Unused time almost never comes back. Act within 48 hours on a charge you did not expect.

  • Didn’t use it — not a refund reason. It’s in the terms.
  • Deleted the app — billing continues until you cancel in the store.
  • Waited a cycle — odds drop fast. Same-day is the window.

What sometimes works

App Store / Play request

Best within 48 hours of a surprise charge.

Same-day support

Some services issue a goodwill refund if you write immediately.

Bank dispute

Only if you never authorized it, or it billed after a confirmed cancel.

Set a reminder two days before renewal. Cancel hub · Pricing.
