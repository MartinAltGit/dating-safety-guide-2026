Dating App Refunds: What Actually Works in 2026

Can you get money back after cancelling a dating subscription? What the app stores and services actually do, and the realistic paths.

The Honest Truth About Refunds

Refunds for unused subscription time are rare, but not impossible. The window is small, and it depends on where you paid and how fast you act. This page is the honest version of what usually works.

What Almost Never Works

Asking for a Refund Because You Didn't Use It

Most terms explicitly state there are no refunds for partially used periods. Simply not using the service is not grounds for a refund.

Deleting the App

Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. You must cancel through the store subscription settings or account settings.

Ignoring a Renewal Until the Next One Hits

The odds of getting a refund drop fast the longer you wait. Act immediately after an unexpected charge.

What Sometimes Works

App Store Refund Requests

Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) and Google Play both have refund flows. Approval is case-by-case and works most often within 48 hours of an unexpected charge.

Same-Day Renewal Follow-Up

Contact the service's support immediately after a charge you did not expect; some issue a goodwill refund for same-day renewals.

Dispute Unauthorized Charges

If you never subscribed, or a charge arrived after a confirmed cancellation, dispute it with your bank or card issuer and keep your cancellation screenshot as evidence.

Prevention Beats Refunds

Set a calendar reminder for 2 days before every renewal or trial-conversion date, and cancel through the store subscription settings the moment you decide you are done.

Cancellation: The Part Most People Skip

Where you paid is where you cancel. On iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions. On web services: account settings, and keep the confirmation email.
