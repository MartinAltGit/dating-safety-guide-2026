Tinder Gold vs Plus vs Platinum: is Gold worth it?

Gold is the likes list. Plus is extra swipes. Platinum is the rest of the upsell. Prices change by country and store. The trap is the long cycle, not the badge.

What is the difference between Tinder Plus, Gold, and Platinum?

Plus: extra likes and rewind. Gold: Plus extras plus who liked you. Platinum: Gold plus priority / paid-message perks in most markets. Feature lists change — read the paywall on your account.

How much does Tinder Gold cost?

There is no global price. US iPhone users often see Plus from about $10 a month on a short term, Gold higher, Platinum higher. Always screenshot your own table in the store — web and phone are different bills.

When Tinder Gold is not worth it

If you wanted a week and they sold you six months. If you will not open the app daily. If you have not found cancel yet. Reddit threads on “is Tinder Gold worth it” are mostly people who forgot auto-renew — that is a billing problem, not a product review.
