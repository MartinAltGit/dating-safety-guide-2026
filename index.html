The 10-Minute Safety Check

Before you pay for any dating app, run through this checklist. It takes 10 minutes and could save you hundreds of dollars and protect your privacy.

Check the Pricing Page

Look for the full price table (not just the intro rate). Check renewal cycles, auto-renewal terms, and cancellation policy. Screenshot it for your records.

Read the Privacy Policy

Verify they allow data deletion, check what data they share with third parties, and understand how long they retain your information.

Test Cancellation

Before paying, find the cancellation path. On iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions. On web: account settings.

Look for Red Flags

Check for fake profiles, pressure to pay immediately, requests for money, or refusal to video chat. These are signs of scams.

Set a Reminder

Set a calendar reminder 2 days before any trial ends or renewal date. This gives you time to cancel before being charged.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if a dating app is safe to pay for? +
Check the pricing page for hidden fees, read the cancellation policy, verify the privacy policy allows data deletion, and look for red flags like pressure to pay immediately or fake profiles. Our safety checklist covers all these points.
What are the biggest red flags in dating apps? +
Fake profiles, pressure to move off-platform quickly, requests for money, poor grammar, and refusal to video chat are major red flags. Always verify identity before meeting or paying.
Can I get a refund from a dating app? +
Refunds are rare but possible. Contact support immediately after an unexpected charge, use app store refund requests within 48 hours, or dispute unauthorized charges with your bank. Prevention is better than refunds.
