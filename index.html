Tinder Gold renews even if you deleted the app

If you bought on iPhone, Apple is the merchant. Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tinder → Cancel. Tinder’s own help says uninstalling the app and deleting your account both leave the subscription running. Cancel the subscription, then delete the account.

Renewal cycle, auto-renew, and the rate after the intro. Tinder Plus, Gold, and Platinum are different products with different bills.

Find cancel in the store that billed you

iPhone: Settings → name → Subscriptions. Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions. Web: the email on the receipt, not the app icon.

Confirm you can delete the account, not just hide it

Pause and “hide me” are not deletion. How to delete a Tinder account is a different door from cancel.

Treat money, crypto, and WhatsApp rushes as the end

Romance scams in 2026 often start on a dating app, then move off-platform, then pitch a crisis or an “investment.” Hub: romance scams. The investment version is pig butchering.

Set a two-day reminder

Cancel in the store. Then open Subscriptions again and confirm Tinder or Bumble is gone. Deleting the icon does nothing.

FAQ

The searches we actually get

How do I delete my Tinder account?

Open Tinder → profile → Settings → Delete account. Cancel Plus, Gold, or Platinum in Apple or Google first. Full taps: delete Tinder account.

How do I cancel Tinder Gold on iPhone?

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tinder → Cancel Subscription. Do it at least a day before renewal. Deleting Tinder does not cancel Gold. Full taps: cancel Tinder subscription.

How do I cancel a Bumble subscription?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Bumble → Cancel Subscription. Same idea on Google Play. Cancel Bumble.

Why didn’t reverse image search catch this profile?

Stolen photos still match. AI-generated faces usually don’t, because they never existed on the web. A blank result is not a green light. Use a live video call with a spontaneous ask — wave a hand, turn 90°, hold up something you name. Spot fakes.

Is online dating safe?

It can be, if you stay on the app until live video, never send money or crypto to a match, meet in public, and cancel subscriptions before you delete the app. Full answer: is online dating safe.

What is a romance scam?

A fake relationship used to extract money — also called romance fraud or a dating scam. Operators move you off-app, then ask for gift cards, wires, crypto, or a fake trading deposit. Definition: what is a romance scam. Hub: romance scams explained. First ask: romance scam scripts.

What are relationship scams?

Romance fraud that uses a romantic relationship — or the appearance of one — to take money. A bad partner can hurt you without running a payment operation. If you have not met and cash, gift cards, or crypto keep leaving, treat it as fraud first. Guide: relationship scams.

They want to move to WhatsApp. Is that a scam?

Not always, but it is the most common next step in a romance scam. Dating apps log reports and can ban accounts. WhatsApp and Telegram don’t. Stay on the app until you’ve done live video. If they push, that’s data. Romance scams · Dating red flags · Scam flags.

What is pig butchering?

A long romance con that ends in a fake crypto or investment site. The “relationship” is the bait. If a match you have not met in person starts talking returns, wallets, or an exclusive platform, stop. Hub: pig butchering scams explained. Dating-app arc: pig butchering on dating apps.

What is sextortion?

Online blackmail that uses intimate images or video — or the threat of a leak — to demand money. Do not pay. Save evidence, stop contact, then report to the platform, FTC ReportFraud, IC3, and local police. Definition: what is sextortion. Playbook: sextortion help.

Can I get a Tinder refund?

Rarely for unused time. Apple and Google review requests; 48 hours is the useful window. Unauthorized charges go to your bank. Tinder refunds.
