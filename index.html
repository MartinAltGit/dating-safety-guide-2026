The 20-second rule

Before paying for any dating service, answer these four questions:

  1. What exactly is the recurring price after any trial or discount ends?
  2. Where is the cancellation button, and does cancelling stop future charges immediately?
  3. Can you hide your profile from search engines and control who sees you?
  4. Does the service have working block and report tools?

If you cannot answer all four, do not pay yet. Find the answers first.
