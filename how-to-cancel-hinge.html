How to cancel a Hinge subscription (Hinge+ and HingeX)

Hinge+ and HingeX auto-renew until you cancel where you paid. Deleting the app does not stop the bill. Cancel in Apple, Google Play, or hinge.co, then confirm the line is gone.

Date Safely editors Updated Aug 19, 2026 18+

Quick answer

To cancel Hinge+ or HingeX on iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Hinge → Cancel Subscription. On Android, use Google Play subscriptions. You keep perks until the paid period ends.

Key takeaways

  • The store line is usually “Hinge,” not “HingeX.”
  • HingeX is the more expensive tier. Cancelling either stops future billing the same way.
  • Uninstalling Hinge does not cancel the subscription.
  • Refunds for unused weeks are rare. Try the store within about 48 hours of a surprise charge.

How to cancel Hinge on iPhone

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Hinge → Cancel Subscription. If Hinge is missing, you paid on Android or on the website.

1. Open Settings

The grey iPhone Settings app, not Hinge.

2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions

Find Hinge. Hinge+ and HingeX both show as Hinge.

3. Cancel Subscription

Confirm. Screenshot it. Do this at least a day before renewal.

How to cancel Hinge on Android

Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Hinge → Cancel subscription.

How to cancel Hinge on the web

Log in at hinge.co → account settings → Subscription → turn off auto-renew. Keep the confirmation.

Does deleting Hinge cancel Hinge+ or HingeX?

No. Cancel in the store first. Same rule as Tinder and Bumble.

FAQ

How do I cancel Hinge+ or HingeX?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Hinge → Cancel Subscription. On Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Hinge → Cancel subscription. On the web: hinge.co account settings → turn off auto-renew.

Does deleting Hinge cancel the subscription?

No. Uninstalling the app leaves Hinge+ or HingeX running in the store. Cancel there first.

Can I get a Hinge refund?

Unused time is rarely refunded. Apple and Google sometimes reverse a surprise charge inside about 48 hours.
