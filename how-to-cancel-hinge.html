How to Cancel Hinge Subscription (Hinge+ and HingeX)

Hinge+ and HingeX renew automatically. Here's how to cancel on every platform and confirm the subscription ends.

Why Cancellation Matters

We verified Hinge's help center and the Apple/Google/web flows. Cancelling keeps your Hinge+ or HingeX perks until the end of the paid period.

Step-by-Step Cancellation

Cancel on iPhone / iOS

Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Hinge → Cancel Subscription → confirm.

Cancel on Android / Google Play

Open Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Hinge → Cancel subscription → confirm.

Cancel on Hinge.com (web)

Log in at hinge.co → account settings → Subscription → turn off Auto-Renew → confirm.

Verify cancellation

Confirm Hinge no longer appears in your active subscriptions, and set a reminder for the renewal date.

What to Know Before You Cancel

Hinge+ vs HingeX

Both auto-renew. HingeX is the more expensive tier. Cancelling stops future billing but you keep perks until the current period ends.

Refund after cancelling

Hinge rarely refunds partially used subscriptions. For unexpected charges, use Apple/Google refund flows within 48 hours — see our dating app refund guide.
