How to report a dating scam before they wipe the chat

Screenshot the profile, the money ask, and the off-app handle. Report in the app first so other people stop matching them. Then tell the bank and, in the US, the FTC.

Updated Aug 17, 202618+ · independent

1. Freeze the evidence

Screenshot the profile URL or username, photos, the chat, wire instructions, wallet addresses, and the WhatsApp number. If they sent a link, copy it into a note — do not open it again. Do not send them one last message to “catch them.” They already know.

2. Report and block in the app

  • Tinder: profile → share icon / three dots → Report. Pick the closest reason (scam, fake, offensive).
  • Bumble: profile → three dots → Report.
  • Hinge: profile → three dots → Report.

Blocking without reporting hides them from you and leaves them in circulation. Do both.

3. Money

Call the bank or card on the statement. Say it was a romance or investment scam if that’s what it was — the script matters for the fraud team. Gift cards and irreversible crypto are often gone; still report the purchase point (Target receipt, Coinbase, Cash App). Never pay a “tax” to release funds. That’s the second bite.

4. Government reports (they compile patterns)

  • US: ReportFraud.ftc.gov and ic3.gov
  • UK: Action Fraud
  • Australia: Scamwatch / ReportCyber

If you sent intimate images and they’re threatening to leak them, that’s sextortion. In the US, report to the FBI IC3 and consider the platform’s revenge-porn flow. Do not pay. Paying does not buy deletion.
