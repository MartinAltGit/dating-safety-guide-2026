Privacy · 18+

Control what they keep

Limit permissions, turn off data sale, and delete the account when you’re done — not just the icon.

Do this

Cut permissions

Most apps do not need contacts or precise GPS.

Tighten visibility

Incognito / private mode if it exists. Limit who can message you.

Opt out of sale

Look for “Do not sell my data” in privacy settings.

Use a spare email

Keep dating mail off your work inbox.

Delete when you leave

Request data deletion. GDPR and CCPA both cover this.

What they collect

  • Profile — name, photos, orientation, job, bio.
  • Location — GPS, IP, sometimes when the app is closed.
  • Usage — swipes, chats, time in-app.
  • Device — IDs used for ads.
  • Messages and photos — stored on their servers.

Extra locks

  • Don’t link Facebook or Instagram.
  • Use a Google Voice number, not your real one.
  • Approximate location, not precise.
  • Read the privacy policy for deletion, not the slogan.
