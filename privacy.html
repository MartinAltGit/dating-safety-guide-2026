Dating App Privacy Checklist: Control What They Collect

What dating services typically collect

  • Precise or approximate location, often continuously while the app is open
  • Photos, chat logs, and in some cases message content for moderation
  • Device identifiers and usage analytics
  • Payment data (usually tokenized via the app store or processor)

The privacy checklist

  1. Use unique photos — never reuse photos from your Instagram/LinkedIn; reverse image search connects your identities instantly.
  2. Turn off distance display if the app allows it; approximate location is enough.
  3. Deny background location — allow location only "while using the app".
  4. Check the profile visibility settings — hide from search engines, hide from people you've blocked, and see if the service shows your profile to non-members.
  5. Use a dedicated email for dating accounts so a breach doesn't touch your main inbox.
  6. Read the data deletion section of the privacy policy before you sign up — you want a real delete, not just deactivation.
