Dating Scam Red Flags 2026: Bots, Catfish, and Romance Scams

How to spot fake profiles, bots, catfish, and romance scams on dating apps. The complete red flags checklist.

Why This Matters

Romance scams cost victims over $1 billion annually. Scammers create fake profiles to build trust, then ask for money, gifts, or personal information. Learn to spot the red flags before it's too late.

Major Red Flags to Watch For

1. Professional-Quality Photos

Scammers often use stolen photos from models or stock photo sites. If the photos look too perfect or professional, be suspicious. Reverse image search the photos to check if they're stolen.

2. Minimal Bio Information

Fake profiles often have very little information in their bio. Real people usually have detailed bios with interests, hobbies, and personal details.

3. Immediate Declarations of Love

Scammers often profess love very quickly, sometimes within days or weeks. This is a manipulation tactic to build trust fast.

4. Requests to Move Off-Platform Quickly

Scammers want to move you to email, text, or WhatsApp quickly to avoid detection by the dating app's safety systems.

5. Refusal to Video Chat

If someone refuses to video chat or always has excuses, they're likely hiding their true identity. Real people are usually willing to video chat.

6. Requests for Money or Gifts

Never send money, gifts, or personal information to someone you've met online. Scammers often claim to have emergencies, be overseas, or in the military.

7. Poor Grammar and Spelling

Many scammers operate from overseas and have poor English skills. While not always a red flag, it's worth noting.

8. Inconsistent Stories

If their stories don't add up or they contradict themselves, they're likely lying. Pay attention to details.

How to Protect Yourself

Verify Identity

Always video chat before meeting or sending money. Ask for specific photos or videos to prove they're real.

Reverse Image Search

Use Google Images or TinEye to reverse search their profile photos. If they appear elsewhere with different names, it's a scam.

Never Send Money

Never send money, gifts, or personal information to someone you've met online, no matter how convincing they are.

Meet in Public

Always meet in public places for the first few dates. Tell a friend where you're going and who you're meeting.

Trust Your Gut

If something feels off, it probably is. Don't ignore red flags or make excuses for suspicious behavior.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I tell if a dating profile is fake? +
Look for professional-quality photos, minimal bio information, immediate declarations of love, requests to move off-platform quickly, and refusal to video chat. Reverse image search profile photos to check if they're stolen.
What is a romance scam? +
A romance scam is when someone creates a fake profile to build a relationship with you, then asks for money, gifts, or personal information. They often claim to be overseas, in the military, or have an emergency.
What should I do if I suspect a scam? +
Stop all communication immediately, do not send money or personal information, report the profile to the dating app, and block the user. If you've already sent money, contact your bank and report it to the FTC.
