The five red flags that cover most scams

  1. Instant intensity — "you're the one" within hours, before any real conversation happened.
  2. Off-platform pressure — pushing you to WhatsApp/Telegram/Snapchat within the first day, before the platform can moderate.
  3. Money or crypto requests — any request for money, gift cards, or crypto investment "opportunities" ends the conversation. No exceptions.
  4. Fake verification sites — links asking you to "verify your identity" on an unknown site are credential phishing. Real verification happens inside the official app only.
  5. Broken video-call excuses — a real person can do a 10-second video call. Endless camera problems is a catfish signal.

What to do when you spot one

  • Use the app's report button — it protects the next person.
  • Block before replying; engagement trains the scammer's scripts.
  • If you already sent money: contact your bank immediately and report to your national fraud authority.
