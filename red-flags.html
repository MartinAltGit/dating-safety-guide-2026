Dating scam red flags: WhatsApp, crypto, and AI faces

The tells you see before money leaves: off-app rushes, no live video, and anyone who wants a wallet.

Date Safely editors Updated Aug 17, 2026 18+

Quick answer

Walk away if they rush you off the app, ask for money or crypto, or will not do live video. Stolen photos still reverse-search. AI faces often don’t. A blank search is not a green light.

Key takeaways

  • Off-app in the first days = they want off the report button.
  • Any wallet, trading screenshot, or “unlock fee” is pig butchering, not a hot tip.
  • Live video on the app, with a stupid physical request.
  • If money already left: bank same day, then FTC and IC3 in the US.

Walk away when you see these

  • Move to WhatsApp or Telegram now — “the app is glitchy” is a script. dating app scams whatsapp is a real query (~30/mo) for a reason.
  • Money, gift cards, or crypto — travel, hospital, visa, “test trade.”
  • An investment platform — pig butchering.
  • No live video — broken camera for a week. A gym clip is not a call. AI / deepfake.
  • Love inside a week — still a script when the grammar is perfect.
  • Blank reverse image search — often generated. Inconclusive.
  • A link to “verify” on another site — fake exclusive apps steal cards.

FAQ

What are the red flags of a dating app scam?

A rush to WhatsApp or Telegram, requests for money or crypto, no live video, “I love you” in a week, and a story that keeps slipping. Stay on the app until you have done an unscripted video call.
