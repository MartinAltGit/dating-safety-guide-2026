How to cancel a Tinder subscription (Gold, Plus, or Platinum)

Plus, Gold, and Platinum auto-renew until you cancel where you paid. Deleting the Tinder app — or deleting the account — does not stop the bill. Cancel in Apple, Google Play, or tinder.com, then confirm the line is gone.

Date Safely editors Updated Aug 19, 2026 18+

Quick answer

To cancel Tinder Gold on iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tinder → Cancel Subscription. On Android, use Google Play subscriptions. Deleting the Tinder app does not cancel Gold, Plus, or Platinum.

Key takeaways

  • Apple bills most iPhone purchases; Google Play bills most Android purchases; Tinder.com bills web.
  • The subscription line is usually labeled “Tinder,” not “Tinder Gold.”
  • Deleting the app or the account does not stop auto-renew — Tinder’s help center says so.
  • You keep paid features until the current period ends. Refunds are rare; 48 hours is the useful store window.

How to cancel a Tinder subscription

Quick answer

Cancel where you paid: iPhone Settings → Subscriptions → Tinder, Google Play subscriptions, or tinder.com auto-renew. Plus, Gold, and Platinum are the same store line. Then delete the account if you want the profile gone.

Google ranks Tinder’s own cancel article first for “how to cancel Tinder subscription.” The gap is a page that also names Gold, iPhone, and the delete-vs-cancel trap in the first screen. You keep paid features until the period ends. A refund request does not stop the next renewal.

How to cancel Tinder Gold on iPhone

Quick answer

Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Tinder → Cancel Subscription. If Tinder is missing from the list, you probably paid on the website or on Android.

1. Open Settings

Not the Tinder app. The grey iPhone Settings app.

2. Tap your name

Apple Account / Apple ID at the top.

3. Tap Subscriptions

Find Tinder. Gold, Plus, and Platinum all show as Tinder.

4. Cancel Subscription

Confirm. You keep Gold until the period ends. Screenshot the confirmation.

This matches Apple’s subscription article and Tinder’s iOS section. Do it at least a day before renewal. “How to cancel Tinder subscription on iPhone” is about 320 US searches a month; “how to cancel Tinder Gold on iPhone” is about 90 — both belong on this page as H2s, not separate thin URLs.

How to cancel Tinder Gold on Android

Quick answer

Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Tinder → Cancel subscription.

If Tinder is not in Play subscriptions, you bought on iPhone or on tinder.com. Cancel there. Switching phones does not move the bill.

How to cancel on Tinder.com

Quick answer

Log in at tinder.com with the email on the receipt → Manage Account → turn off Auto-Renew. Keep the email.

Web prices are often different from the App Store. The cancel door is different too. Receipts that start with TNDR or COSU are Tinder’s web/card flow, not Apple.

Does deleting Tinder cancel the subscription?

Quick answer

No. Uninstalling the app does not cancel Gold. Deleting the Tinder account does not cancel Gold either. Tinder’s help center states both facts. Cancel in the store, then delete if you want.

Related: how to delete a Tinder account is a different door from cancel. Do cancel first.

Can I get a Tinder Gold refund?

Quick answer

Rarely for unused weeks. Ask Apple or Google within about 48 hours of a surprise charge. Card and PayPal web purchases go through Tinder’s refund form with the order number.

FAQ

How do I cancel Tinder Gold on iPhone?

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tinder → Cancel Subscription. Confirm. Do this at least 24 hours before renewal. The line in Subscriptions usually says “Tinder,” not “Tinder Gold.”

Does deleting Tinder cancel my subscription?

No. Tinder’s help center says uninstalling the app and deleting the account both leave Plus, Gold, Platinum, and memberships running. Cancel in Apple, Google Play, or Tinder.com — whichever billed you.

How do I cancel Tinder Gold on Android?

Open the Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Tinder → Cancel subscription.

Can I get a Tinder Gold refund?

Unused time is almost never refunded. Apple and Google review requests; the useful window is about 48 hours after an unexpected charge. Unauthorized charges go to your bank.

How do I cancel Tinder Plus or Platinum?

Same path as Gold. Apple and Google list one Tinder subscription. Cancel that line and auto-renew stops for the paid tier you bought.
