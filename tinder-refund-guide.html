Refunds · Tinder

Tinder refunds: what actually works

Tinder’s terms generally do not refund unused subscription time. The realistic paths are an app-store request within 48 hours of a surprise charge, or a bank dispute if you never authorized it.

Overview Cancel Tinder Tinder refund Delete account All refunds

Cancel first

A refund request does not stop the next renewal. Cancel Tinder in the same store you paid, then screenshot confirmation.

Ask Apple or Google within 48 hours

Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play: Payments & subscriptions → Budget & order history → the charge → Request a refund. Same-day unexpected renewals are the most common approvals.

Write Tinder support only after that

In-app help can issue a goodwill credit; it is slower and less reliable than the store. Keep your cancellation screenshot.

Dispute unauthorized charges

If you never subscribed, or a charge arrived after a confirmed cancel, dispute with the card issuer. “I didn’t use it” is not unauthorized.

Deleting the app is not a refund path

Removing Tinder from the phone does not cancel Plus, Gold, or Platinum and does not reverse a charge. Deleting the profile is a separate step.
