Before you pay

Find the full price table

Usually in terms or FAQ — not the paywall tile.

Check the renewal cycle

Weekly, monthly, or a 3–6 month lump.

Note the trial conversion date

Reminder two days before it flips to paid.

Test cancel first

Store subscriptions vs web account settings.

Assume no refund

Unused time is almost never paid back.

Common traps

  • Intro bait — first month looks cheap, then 2–3×.
  • Long cycles — a “small” weekly plan billed as six months at once.
  • Auto-renew on — default everywhere. You have to turn it off.
  • Silent trials — 7 days, then a charge if you forget.
