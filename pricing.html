The sticker price is rarely the price you pay. Most services run an introductory rate that steps up after 1–3 months, auto-renew by default, and bill in longer cycles (3 or 6 months) than the one shown on the first screen. This checklist helps you understand what you're actually paying for.

How Dating Subscriptions Actually Bill You

The sticker price is rarely the price you pay. Most services run an introductory rate that steps up after 1–3 months, auto-renew by default, and bill in longer cycles (3 or 6 months) than the one shown on the first screen.

Find the Full Price Table

Usually in terms or FAQ, not on the paywall screen. Screenshot it for your records. Look for the standard rate, not the introductory rate.

Check the Renewal Cycle

"Weekly", "monthly" and "3-month" plans renew at that same interval. A "small" weekly charge can become a big 6-month charge if you're not careful.

Check the Trial Conversion Date

Set a calendar reminder 2 days before it converts. Free trials auto-convert to paid plans unless you cancel before the conversion date.

Test Cancellation Before Paying

On app-store installs, cancel through the store subscription settings; on web, cancel in account settings and confirm you get a cancellation email.

Know the Refund Policy

Most dating services do not refund unused time after cancellation. Read the refund policy before paying to avoid surprises.

Common Pricing Traps to Avoid

1. Introductory Rate Bait-and-Switch

Many apps show a low "first month" price that jumps to 2-3x the rate after the initial period. Always check the standard rate before signing up.

2. Long Billing Cycles

Some apps push 3, 6, or 12-month plans that renew all at once. A "small" weekly charge can become a large lump-sum charge every 6 months.

3. Auto-Renewal by Default

Unless you turn it off before the renewal date, subscriptions keep charging on the same card. Auto-renewal is almost always on by default.

4. Silent Trial Conversions

A 7-day free trial flips into a paid plan unless you cancel 2 days before it ends. Many people forget and get charged unexpectedly.

Cancellation: The Part Nobody Reads

Deleting the app does NOT cancel the subscription. On iOS go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. On Android, Play Store → Payments & subscriptions. On web services, cancel inside account settings and keep the confirmation email.

Prevention Beats Refunds

Set a calendar reminder for 2 days before every renewal, and cancel through the store subscription settings the moment you decide you're done — not when the next charge hits. This simple habit can save you hundreds of dollars.
