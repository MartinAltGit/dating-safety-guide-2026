How dating subscriptions actually bill you

The sticker price is rarely the price you pay. Most services run an introductory rate that steps up after 1–3 months, auto-renew by default, and bill in longer cycles (3 or 6 months) than the one shown on the first screen.

The pricing checklist

  1. Find the full price table — usually in terms or FAQ, not on the paywall screen. Screenshot it.
  2. Check the renewal cycle — "weekly", "monthly" and "3-month" plans renew at that same interval.
  3. Check the trial conversion date — set a calendar reminder 2 days before it converts.
  4. Test cancellation before paying — on app-store installs, cancel through the store subscription settings; on web, cancel in account settings and confirm you get a cancellation email.
  5. Know the refund policy — most dating services do not refund unused time after cancellation.

Cancellation: the part nobody reads

Deleting the app does NOT cancel the subscription. On iOS go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. On Android, Play Store → Payments & subscriptions. On web services, cancel inside account settings and keep the confirmation email.
