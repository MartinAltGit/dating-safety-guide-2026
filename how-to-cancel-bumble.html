How to Cancel Bumble Subscription (iPhone, Android, Web)

Bumble Boost and Bumble Premium auto-renew. Here's exactly how to cancel on every platform and make sure the charges stop.

Why Cancellation Matters

We verified Bumble's official help center and the Apple, Google Play, and web flows. Cancelling correctly means the subscription ends at the next billing period, not immediately.

Step-by-Step Cancellation

Cancel on iPhone / iOS

Open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Bumble → Cancel Subscription → confirm. Best done 24 hours before renewal.

Cancel on Android / Google Play

Open Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Bumble → Cancel subscription → confirm.

Cancel on Bumble.com (web)

Log in → settings → Manage Subscription → toggle Auto-Renew off → confirm. Save the confirmation.

Verify cancellation

Re-open the store subscriptions page to confirm Bumble is no longer active. Set a reminder for the renewal date.

What to Know Before You Cancel

Bumble Boost vs Premium

Both are auto-renewing subscriptions. Cancelling stops future charges but you keep Boost/Premium features until the end of the current billing period you've paid for.

Refund after cancelling

Bumble generally does not refund partially used periods. If you were charged by mistake, see our Bumble refund guide or use Apple/Google refund flows within 48 hours.
