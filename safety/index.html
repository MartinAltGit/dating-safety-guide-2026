Tinder Gold renews even if you deleted the app

If you bought on iPhone, Apple is the merchant. Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tinder → Cancel Subscription. Same idea on Google Play. Tinder’s own help says uninstalling the app and deleting your account both leave the subscription running. We wrote the taps: cancel Tinder Gold.

Find cancel in the store that billed you

iPhone: Settings → name → Subscriptions. Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions. Web: the email on the receipt, not the app icon.

Confirm you can delete the account, not just hide it

Pause and “hide me” are not deletion. Look for a delete-account path before you upload ID or a selfie.

Treat money, crypto, and WhatsApp rushes as the end

Romance scams in 2026 often start on a dating app, then move off-platform, then pitch an “investment.” Pig butchering is the name for that con.

FAQ

The searches we actually get

How do I cancel Tinder Gold on iPhone?

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tinder → Cancel Subscription. Do it at least a day before renewal. Deleting Tinder does not cancel Gold. Full taps: cancel Tinder.

Why didn’t reverse image search catch this profile?

Stolen photos still match. AI-generated faces usually don’t, because they never existed on the web. A blank result is not a green light. Use a live video call with a spontaneous ask — wave a hand, turn 90°, hold up something you name. Spot fakes.

They want to move to WhatsApp. Is that a scam?

Not always, but it is the most common next step in a romance scam. Dating apps log reports and can ban accounts. WhatsApp and Telegram don’t. Stay on the app until you’ve done live video. If they push, that’s data. Red flags.

What is pig butchering?

A long romance con that ends in a fake crypto or investment site. The “relationship” is the bait. If a match you have not met in person starts talking returns, wallets, or an exclusive platform, stop. How the con works.

Can I get a Tinder refund?

Rarely for unused time. Apple and Google review requests; 48 hours is the useful window. Unauthorized charges go to your bank. Tinder refunds.
